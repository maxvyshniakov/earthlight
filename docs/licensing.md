# Code licensing decision

The repository is currently private. No public reuse license has been granted;
`package.json` remains `UNLICENSED` until the final license text is adopted.

Requirements selected on 10 October 2026:

- Permit commercial use, including paid products and client projects.
- Permit embedding in proprietary products and keeping modifications private.
- Require a visible, clickable author credit next to the rendered scene for as
  long as the scene is displayed. A notice hidden in About, Credits, source
  files or a license bundle is insufficient.
- Do not require recipients to publish their changes to the renderer.

Proposed credit: `Earthlight by Max Vyshniakov`, linking to the author's
personal site. Exact placement, contrast, small-screen behavior and treatment
of derived renderers need to be specified in the final license.

MIT alone does not impose this visible-link requirement. Do not label a license
with additional restrictive terms as MIT. Review a dedicated attribution
license before opening the source; do not infer that these notes grant rights.

Third-party data and dependencies retain their own terms. See [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).

References:

- [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit)
- [MPL FAQ](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/)
- [CPAL section 14](https://opensource.org/license/cpal-1.0) permits attribution
  at launch and does not by itself express the desired persistent placement.
